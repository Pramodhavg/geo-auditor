Your fix plan, in order

Ranked by impact ÷ effort — do these top-down. The ones at the top are the cheapest, biggest wins.

1
Organization schema exists but is thin
Impact: Medium Effort: Easy

AI engines use structured data to know who you are and what you're known for. A name-and-URL stub isn't enough to build an entity around you. `sameAs` (links to your LinkedIn, socials, listings) is how they connect the dots.

Do this: Complete your Organization schema. Example:

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "[Business Name]",
  "url": "https://www.sweetgreen.com",
  "description": "[One clear sentence about what you do and for whom]",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://www.linkedin.com/company/[you]",
    "https://www.instagram.com/[you]"
  ]
}
</script>
2
No FAQ / question-answer content
Impact: Medium Effort: Easy

People ask AI engines full questions ('what's the best X in Y for Z?'). Pages that answer those questions directly, in self-contained blocks, get pulled into answers.

Do this: Add an FAQ answering the real questions your customers ask, one clear answer each. Mark it up with FAQPage schema.

3
Very few concrete statistics (3 across your pages)
Impact: High Effort: Moderate

AI engines quote specific, verifiable facts. Pages that say 'we help many clients save money' get skipped; pages that say 'we've cut invoice processing time 63% for 40+ SMBs' get cited. The Princeton GEO study found adding statistics lifts AI visibility by up to 40%.

Do this: Rewrite vague claims as numbers. Find your 5 strongest results and quantify them. Before → After example:

BEFORE:  "We help small businesses save time on invoicing."
AFTER:   "We cut invoice processing time by 63% on average across 40+ SMB clients, from ~12 minutes to under 5 per invoice (2025 data)."
4
Content cites no external authority sources
Impact: Medium Effort: Moderate

Counter-intuitively, linking OUT to credible sources makes AI engines more likely to cite YOU — it signals your claims are grounded, not marketing. (Princeton GEO: 'Cite Sources' was the single most consistent visibility booster.)

Do this: Back your key claims with a link to a credible source (industry report, standards body, research).

5
No llms.txt file
Impact: Low Effort: Easy

llms.txt is an emerging standard (adopted by Anthropic, Stripe, Cloudflare) that hands AI models a plain-English index of your key pages. Cost to add is near zero.

Do this: Create llms.txt at your site root. A starter, fill in the brackets:

# [Business Name]

> [One sentence: what you do and who you serve.]

## Key pages
- [About](https://www.sweetgreen.com/about): who we are
- [Services](https://www.sweetgreen.com/services): what we offer
- [Contact](https://www.sweetgreen.com/contact): how to reach us
6
No quotations found
Impact: Low Effort: Moderate

Quotes from named experts/clients are a proven citation booster and add trust.

Do this: Add 1–2 short, attributed quotes (a client result, an expert take) to key pages.
