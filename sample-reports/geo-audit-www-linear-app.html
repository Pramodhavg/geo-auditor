No Organization schema
Impact: Medium Effort: Easy

Without structured identity data, AI engines have to guess who you are from prose. Businesses with clear Organization schema are far easier to attribute and recommend.

Do this: Add Organization (or LocalBusiness) JSON-LD to your homepage <head>:

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "[Business Name]",
  "url": "https://www.linear.app",
  "description": "[One clear sentence about what you do and for whom]",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://www.linkedin.com/company/[you]",
    "https://www.instagram.com/[you]"
  ]
}
</script>
